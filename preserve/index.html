Grew more than you can eat? Here is how to keep it

Growing food is only half the job. Storing it is how a backyard actually lowers your grocery bill through the winter.

Simple ways to store your harvest

  • Cool, dark storage: potatoes, onions, garlic, carrots, beets and winter squash keep for months in the right conditions.
  • Separate them: keep apples away from potatoes, and onions away from potatoes too.
  • Drying: herbs, beans and peppers dry easily with air or a dehydrator.
  • Canning: follow a tested recipe. Low-acid foods like beans need a pressure canner.
  • Freezing: blanch most vegetables briefly before freezing to keep quality.

Root cellar without a basement?

A root cellar does not have to be a big underground room. People use a buried container, an unheated corner of a garage, or an insulated box. The goal is cool temperature, steady humidity and darkness.
