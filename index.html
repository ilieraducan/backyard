Rising grocery prices make a lot of people think about a garden. But most beginners don't know where to start: what to plant, how much space they need, and how to keep it going all year.

What The Self-Sufficient Backyard is

The Self-Sufficient Backyard is a guide about turning an ordinary backyard into a small, productive homestead. According to the seller, it covers growing vegetables, saving water, storing food and other step-by-step projects for a more self-reliant home.

Who it is good for

  • Beginners who want a clear plan instead of random gardening tips.
  • Families who want to grow some of their own food and save money.
  • Anyone interested in homesteading, preparedness or a simpler lifestyle.

What to keep in mind

  • A guide gives you the plan, but the garden still needs your time and work.
  • Results depend on your climate, soil and space. Start small.
  • Check what format you are buying (printed book or digital) on the seller's page.
