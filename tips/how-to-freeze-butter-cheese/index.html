How to Freeze Butter & Cheese

Holiday sales are the best time to stock up on butter and cheese, and both freeze well if you wrap them right. Hard cheese turns a little crumbly after freezing, so save it for casseroles, soups and baking.

The full list

  1. Freeze butter in its wrapper
  2. Overwrap in foil or a bag
  3. Butter keeps 6-9 months
  4. Cut hard cheese into 8 oz
  5. Shred some before freezing
  6. Thaw both in the fridge
  7. Cook with thawed cheese
