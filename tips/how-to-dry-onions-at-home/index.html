How to Dry Onions at Home

Dried onion flakes take up a fraction of the space of fresh onions and keep for many months in a dark cupboard. Old USDA bulletins on drying vegetables used the same basics: thin, even slices, steady low heat and good airflow.

The full list

  1. Peel, trim tops and roots
  2. Slice evenly, 1/4 inch thick
  3. Spread in a single layer
  4. Dry at 130-140°F till brittle
  5. Dry in a garage, it's strong
  6. Cool, then pack airtight
  7. Grind some into onion powder
