Old-Time Crock Sauerkraut

Long before freezers, farm families put up the fall cabbage crop as sauerkraut in a stoneware crock. Salt draws out the juice, and the cabbage ferments under its own brine. Here is the old method in our own words, with the salt ratio and temperatures from the USDA Complete Guide to Home Canning.

The full list

  1. Shred firm, fresh cabbage
  2. 3 Tbsp canning salt per 5 lb
  3. Pack until juice covers it
  4. Weigh it down under brine
  5. Keep at 70-75°F
  6. Ferment about 3-4 weeks
  7. Toss if slimy or smells off
