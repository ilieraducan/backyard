How to Pressure Can Sweet Potatoes

Home-canned sweet potatoes are ready for a quick side dish or pie filling all winter. They are low-acid, so they must be pressure canned; these steps follow the USDA Complete Guide to Home Canning.

The full list

  1. Boil whole 15-20 min, peel
  2. Cut into 1-inch pieces
  3. Never mash or puree them
  4. Hot pack, cover with liquid
  5. Leave 1-inch headspace
  6. Pints 65 min, quarts 90 min
  7. 10 lb weighted, 11 lb dial
