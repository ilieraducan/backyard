Best Cold-Hardy Chicken Breeds

If your winters are cold, start with breeds that are heavy, well-feathered and have small combs that resist frostbite. These classic dual-purpose hens keep going when the water bucket freezes.

The full list

  1. Wyandotte: small rose comb
  2. Plymouth Rock: steady layer
  3. Buff Orpington: fluffy, calm
  4. Australorp: lays in the cold
  5. Brahma: big, feathered feet
  6. Chantecler: bred for Canada
  7. Small combs resist frostbite
