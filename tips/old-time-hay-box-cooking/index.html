Old-Time Hay Box Cooking

A century ago, USDA Farmers' Bulletin 771 taught families to finish cooking in a "fireless cooker": an insulated box that holds a pot's heat for hours. It saves fuel and still works when the power is out. Here is the method in our own words.

The full list

  1. Boil food hard before boxing
  2. Use a heavy pot, tight lid
  3. Nest it in hay, wool or quilts
  4. Pack insulation all around
  5. Don't open it while it cooks
  6. Best for oats, beans, stews
  7. Serve while still steaming hot
