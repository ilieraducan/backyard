How to Pressure Can Turkey

Turkey can be home canned the same way as chicken and other poultry, but only in a pressure canner. These steps follow the hot-pack poultry directions in the USDA Complete Guide to Home Canning, rewritten in our own words. Always check the pressure table for your altitude and gauge type.

The full list

  1. Use a pressure canner only
  2. Cook meat about 2/3 done
  3. Pack hot, add hot broth
  4. Leave 1 1/4 inch headspace
  5. Boned: pints 75, quarts 90 min
  6. 10 lb weight gauge to 1,000 ft
  7. Adjust pressure for altitude
