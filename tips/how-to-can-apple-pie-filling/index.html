How to Can Apple Pie Filling

Home-canned apple pie filling means a pie is only a crust away all winter. These are the key safety points from the USDA Complete Guide to Home Canning.

The full list

  1. Use ClearJel, not flour
  2. Blanch slices 1 minute
  3. Fill jars hot, 1-inch space
  4. Remove air bubbles
  5. Water bath 25 min (0–1,000 ft)
  6. Add time at higher altitude
  7. Follow a tested recipe
