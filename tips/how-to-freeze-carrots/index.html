How to Freeze Carrots

Frozen carrots keep their color and sweetness for months if you blanch them first. Blanching stops the enzymes that make frozen vegetables tough and dull.

The full list

  1. Pick young, tender carrots
  2. Wash, peel and trim tops
  3. Slice or dice about 1/4 inch
  4. Blanch slices 2 minutes
  5. Small whole carrots: 5 min
  6. Chill in ice water, drain
  7. Pack with 1/2-inch headspace
