How to Pressure Can Soup

Home-canned soup is a lifesaver on a cold, busy night, but it has to be done the tested way. These steps follow the soup procedure in the USDA Complete Guide to Home Canning: pressure canning only, no thickeners or pasta, and add noodles or rice when you heat a jar to serve.

The full list

  1. Pressure canner only
  2. No noodles, rice or flour
  3. Cook dry beans before adding
  4. Fill jars half with solids
  5. Top with broth, 1 in space
  6. Pints 60 min, quarts 75 min
  7. Weighted: 10 lb up to 1,000 ft
