Make a Seed Starting Calendar

Winter is the time to plan when each crop gets started indoors. Count back from your average last frost date and you will know exactly which seeds to sow in January, February and March, with no rushing and no leggy seedlings.

The full list

  1. Find your last frost date
  2. Onions: 8-12 weeks before
  3. Peppers: 8-10 weeks before
  4. Tomatoes: 6-8 weeks before
  5. Cabbage family: 4-6 weeks
  6. Squash: 3-4 weeks, or direct
  7. Write every date on a calendar
