The Oldest Herbal

155 Plant Remedies from the Ebers Papyrus, Ancient Egypt c. 1550 BC (Translated and Annotated)

The Ebers Papyrus is a medical scroll about 18.6 metres long, the largest complete one to survive from ancient Egypt. This book collects its purely plant-based remedies:

  • Honey for burns and wounds, figs and dates for the belly
  • Frankincense and myrrh in ointments and dressings
  • The "Book of the Degam Tree", a 3,500-year-old list of uses for the castor-oil plant
  • A guide to 31 ancient plants, with clear safety warnings for the dangerous ones

A work of history, not a medical guide. Read, don't try.

The Oldest Advice in the World

The 43 teachings of Ptahhotep, Egypt, about 4,000 years ago

Written by a chief minister for his son, these are some of the oldest life lessons ever put on paper:

  • Stay teachable: no one ever reaches the limit of a skill
  • Listen more than you speak, and don't repeat angry words
  • Don't let work swallow your life
