Home canning & preserving

Canning turns a summer harvest into food for the whole winter. There are two methods: a water bath for high-acid foods (jams, pickles, tomatoes with added acid) and a pressure canner for low-acid foods (green beans, corn, soups, meat).

Safety first: USDA guidance says low-acid vegetables and meats must be pressure canned. A water bath alone is not safe for them.
