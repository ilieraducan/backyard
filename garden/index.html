Your first vegetable garden: start with one raised bed

Most first gardens fail because they start too big. One well-planned raised bed is enough to put fresh food on your table this season.

A simple beginner plan

  • Size: one bed about 4 x 8 ft (120 x 240 cm), 10–12 in (25–30 cm) deep.
  • Spot: at least 6 hours of direct sun, close to a water source.
  • Soil: mix good topsoil with compost instead of using only garden dirt.
  • First crops: lettuce, radishes, bush beans, zucchini, cherry tomatoes and herbs.
  • Water: deeply and less often, in the morning. Mulch to keep moisture in.

Easy crops that forgive mistakes

  • Radishes: ready in about 4 weeks.
  • Leaf lettuce: cut it and it grows back.
  • Bush beans: no trellis needed.
  • Zucchini: one or two plants feed a family.
  • Herbs like basil, parsley and chives: save money right away.
